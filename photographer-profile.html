About Marcus

Marcus Reid is a New York-based wedding and portrait photographer with over 9 years of experience capturing the authentic emotion behind life's most meaningful moments. His signature style blends photojournalistic storytelling with artful composition — resulting in images that feel both cinematic and deeply personal.

Having photographed over 320 weddings across the US, Europe, and Asia, Marcus brings a calm, reassuring presence to every shoot. He believes the best photographs happen when people forget the camera is there, and his clients often comment on how natural and relaxed they felt throughout their sessions.

Marcus offers full-day wedding coverage, engagement sessions, bridal portraits, and intimate elopement packages. He delivers edited images within 4–6 weeks of the event.
