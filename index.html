FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using FrameFolio.

How do I find a photographer in my city?
Simply go to the Browse page and use the city filter to narrow down photographers in your area. You can also filter by specialty to find the exact style you're looking for.
Are all photographers on FrameFolio verified?
Yes. Every photographer who submits their profile goes through a manual review process where we evaluate their portfolio quality, professional background, and client references before approval.
How do I contact a photographer?
Visit any photographer's profile page and use the enquiry form. You can describe your project, preferred dates, and budget. The photographer will respond directly to your email.
I'm a photographer — how do I list my services?
Head to the "List Your Services" page and submit your photographer profile. Include your portfolio images, specialties, city, and pricing. Our team will review and approve your listing within 2–3 business days.
Is there a fee for listing on FrameFolio?
We offer a free basic listing plan and two premium plans with enhanced features like priority placement, portfolio galleries, and spotlight features. See our pricing on the List Your Services page.
